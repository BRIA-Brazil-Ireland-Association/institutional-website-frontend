About the research

Where does this data come from?

Between October 2024 and July 2026, 1,897 Brazilian professionals living in Ireland answered the BRIA survey. They told us about their training, their careers and their life here. This report reads those answers one step at a time.

Participation was voluntary, so the results describe who responded, not the whole Brazilian community in Ireland.
